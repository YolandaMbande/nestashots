How it started

My Camera
+ Me.

What started as a hobby became something much bigger.

Welcome! I am a passionate photographer specialising in various genres. My journey in photography started from a young age, and over time I developed a keen eye for capturing moments that tell a story. I love working with clients to create beautiful memories that last a lifetime.

I am a professional wedding photographer in King Williams Town, while also specialising in pregnancy shoots, birthdays, couple engagements and live music performances.

I studied Human Resources as a profession, but photography began as a hobby. After receiving encouragement and praise from people who saw my work, I decided to turn that passion into a business. Through passion and consistency, I found growth in the art within a short space of time and I plan to keep growing.

“I believe in capturing people's special moments in their purest form - in a way that tells a story like a never-ending movie.”
